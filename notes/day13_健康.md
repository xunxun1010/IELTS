语段四：健康与现代生活方式 (Health & Modern Lifestyle)  
"In recent years, the prevalence of diet-related diseases has become a pressing issue in many modern societies. This alarming trend is primarily driven by the overconsumption of fast food and the widespread adoption of a sedentary lifestyle. Because people are often overwhelmed by their demanding work schedules, they tend to rely on convenient but nutritionally deficient meals. To tackle this problem, it is imperative for governments to launch public awareness campaigns promoting a balanced diet. Furthermore, individuals must take the initiative to incorporate regular physical activity into their daily routines, which is crucial for maintaining overall well-being."

中文释义：
近年来，与饮食相关的疾病的普遍化已经成为许多现代社会中一个紧迫的问题。这种令人担忧的趋势主要是由于过度食用快餐和久坐不动的生活方式的普及所驱动的。因为人们经常被繁重的工作日程所压垮，他们倾向于依赖方便但营养缺乏的食物。为了解决这个问题，政府必须发起旨在推广均衡饮食的公众意识宣传活动。此外，个人也必须主动将规律的体育活动纳入日常生活中，这对于保持整体健康至关重要。
